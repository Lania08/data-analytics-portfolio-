Key findings

  1. 1

    Verification is the bottleneck Primary-source verification averages 35.8 days — about 6× the next slowest step. If you fix one thing in this process, it's this step.

  2. 2

    One check causes most of the wait The State Medical License check is the slowest single verification item at 24.1 days on average. The NPDB query, by comparison, comes back in 4.5 days.

  3. 3

    Specialty changes the wait by three weeks OB/GYN applications take the longest start to finish (91.0 days). Family Medicine is fastest at 69.6 days.

  4. 4

    15 applications are stuck and need attention Each has sat in one step for 30+ days. Most — 10 of the 15 — are waiting at Completeness Review, an early step in the process. The single longest wait is one file that has sat at Peer References for 95 days.
